# Roblox storefront media: rules and sources (October 2026)

## Signals this media affects

- **Play-through rate** (top-priority positive signal): how often an impression in Recommended for You becomes a play. Replaced "qualified play-through rate" in 2026. Official: Creator Hub Discovery page; DevForum "Recommended For You Algorithm Improvements That Better Value Long-Term Retention".
- **First-play bounce rate** (negative signal), split into under 60 seconds and 61–180 seconds. Same sources.
- Roblox's stated reason for the change: looking only at the click could favour games that win attention with exciting thumbnails but do not keep players (reported wording; intent confirmed in the DevForum post).

## Gameplay video on Home

- Fully rolled out inside Recommended for You on Home. Upload in Creator Hub under place settings, Videos tab; the video is eligible to play once approved. Official: DevForum "Gameplay Videos on Home: Help Your Games Get Discovered".
- Case studies in that post: Notoriety: A PAYDAY Experience, +41% RFY plays and +39% RFY playtime with the video present; Superstar Baseball, +31% playtime. These are single-game lifts, not platform averages.
- Content rules as creators report them: real, current gameplay; no cinematic trailer; no voice-over or narration; graphics no better than what players actually see. Roblox's thumbnail documentation requires footage to show real gameplay; the specific prohibitions were not verified verbatim, so check the Videos tab guidance before a dispute.
- Standout Games nominations also ask for gameplay video that highlights the core mechanics (Creator Hub, Standout Games, updated 16 October 2026).

## Moments

- Moments is Roblox's short-form gameplay feed with a Join button, launched September 2025 from Search and the More tab. The Moments tab arrived with the 2026 app redesign, rolling out from 28 July 2026 starting in Canada, New Zealand and Singapore. Clips in Moments are player-captured, so a developer's uploaded video should look like one.

## Icon and 16:9 thumbnail

- Icon: square, shown as small as ~150 px on Home. Thumbnails: 16:9 (1920×1080 recommended), shown larger with more detail.
- Creator claim (not found in official docs): search results show the first 16:9 image from the gallery instead of the icon, so the first thumbnail does the icon's job in search.
- Changing the icon to chase clicks mid-launch breaks the promise test; keep icon and thumbnail telling the same story.
